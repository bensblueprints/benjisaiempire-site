Three small moves. None of them are dialing.

Tuesday is the first dial day. Use the weekend to load the gun.

1

Print the Master Script

The PDF is in the welcome email. Print it on real paper. Tape it next to your phone. Don't read it off a screen on Day 1 — the eye drift kills your tone. Paper, taped, in your line of sight while you dial.

2

Listen to the 30-Second Opener twice

The mp3 is in the welcome email. Three takes, three different cadences. Listen on commute, walk, or before bed. Note the pauses. Note where I shut up. Most first-time callers fail because they pitch too soon — the audio fixes that.

3

Run the AI prospecting prompt

Drop the prompt into Claude or ChatGPT. Get your first 25 names. Verify the top 5 on LinkedIn — AI hallucinates names sometimes, you always verify before dialing. That's your week 1 dial list. 5 prospects per day for 5 weekdays.

4

Block 30 minutes a day on your calendar

Same time every weekday for 30 days. Title it: "5 Dials." Make it recurring. 9am works. 11am works. After lunch works. The TIME doesn't matter. The CONSISTENCY does. Reply to the welcome email with the time you picked.
