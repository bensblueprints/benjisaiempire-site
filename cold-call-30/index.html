The 30-day rhythm

Here's what every weekday looks like.

Same shape, every day, for 30 days. The work is repetitive on purpose — that's how the muscle gets built.

  • Morning email (5 min read). The day's tactical lesson + the move. No filler.
  • 5 dials before noon. Use the script. Log the result. Voicemail counts as a dial; gatekeeper-blocked counts as a dial.
  • Daily accountability post in #cold-call-challenge. "5 done" or "0 — here's what got in the way." Members posting in that channel finish at 4x the rate of those who don't.
  • Tuesday Cold Call Live (free YouTube). I run the same script on real prospects, live. Watch one a week to reinforce the cadence.

Weekends are rest + prep. Day 6 is the Opener rewrite. Day 13 is the halfway-point real-talk. Day 30 is graduation + Cold Caller badge.

    Is this really free?

    Yes. Master Script PDF, 30-Second Opener audio, AI prospecting prompt, 30 daily emails, community channel access — all free. Day 30 graduation includes a 24-hour Builder upgrade window at the founder rate ($99/mo locked for life). Take it, don't take it — either way the script is yours.

    What if I can't dial 5 a day?

    Then this isn't for you, and that's a fair filter. The challenge has no penalty for missing days — you can pick up where you are — but the badge is for the people who actually finish 100 dials in 30 days. If 5 dials a day feels impossible, the broader 30-Day AI Empire Challenge is the same daily-action format with lower friction (5 minutes a day, not 5 dials).

    What does the "Master Script PDF download" actually look like?

    One-page printable PDF. Top half: the 30-Second Opener verbatim, plus the Hook framework. Middle: 5 Discovery Questions in order — current approach / what's working / what you've tried / magic-wand fix / who else is involved. Bottom half: 5 Objection Categories (too expensive, think about it, partner, not now, already working with someone) with the Acknowledge → Clarify → Reframe framework that handles all five. Plus the Assumed Close. Tape it next to your phone.

    I'm new to cold calling. Is this for beginners?

    Yes — if you're willing to actually pick up the phone. The script is beginner-proof. The Day 0 audio walks you through the cadence. Day 2 is one dial, no script — just to break the inertia. By Day 8 you're running Discovery Questions. By Day 24 you're running the Assumed Close. The structure carries you. The dials are still on you.

    What if I miss a day or three?

    Pick up where you are. The sequence keeps moving on its 24-hour cadence. If you miss 5 days in a row with no email engagement and no community posts, the system pauses the sequence and sends a single re-engagement email. Reply, the sequence resumes. If you don't post a single dial count by Day 30, the badge isn't issued — the system needs at least one self-reported tally to confirm the 100.

    Why the daily community post?

    Members posting daily in #cold-call-challenge finish at roughly 4x the rate of members who don't. The accountability does the heavy lifting. You're not posting for me — you're posting for your future self in 30 days. Format: "Day N: 5 dials done" or "Day N: 0, here's what got in the way." Both count.

    What about the Hardcore badge?

    If you complete BOTH the 30-Day Cold Calling Challenge AND the 30-Day AI Empire Challenge, you earn the rare Hardcore badge. The system checks for both completion tags and auto-applies it. Most people finish neither. Some finish one. Hardcore is for the operators who finish both.
