The suggested path

4 weeks. 7 modules. One designed Empire by Day 30.

Don't try to consume Empire OS in one sitting — the material is meta and rewards sleeping on it. Here's the 4-week pace I recommend, with one decision artifact per week. By Day 30 you have your own version of the OS on paper.

Week 1

The thesis & the stack (Modules 1-2)

Sit with the parallel-growth thesis. Open the Year 1 milestone math spreadsheet. Plug your own numbers in. Decide what your tier stack looks like — do you have an Insider tier? A founder cohort? Where does the gold-crown gravity live in your business?

  • Watch Modules 1 + 2 (~2 hours)
  • Fill in the milestone math spreadsheet
  • Decide your tier stack (Insider / Builder / Founder analog)
Start Week 1 →
Week 2

The content factory (Modules 3-4)

The cadence IS the brand. Pick your two open public streams and your one gated one. Sketch your milestone content moments — the $X MRR posts you'll publish in the next 12 months. By end of week, you have a calendar.

  • Watch Modules 3 + 4 (~2 hours)
  • Pick your weekly live cadence (3 shows)
  • Draft the 5-year milestone content map
Continue to Week 2 →
Week 3

Launch Week + first hire (Modules 5-6)

Open the Launch Week run-sheets. Pick a quarterly launch window (Q+1) and put it on the calendar. Decide which role you'll hire first when you cross $25K MRR — editor/VA or cold-call closer? Draft the role description.

  • Watch Modules 5 + 6 (~2 hours)
  • Block a 5-day Launch Week on the calendar
  • Draft your first-hire role description
Continue to Week 3 →
Week 4

The honest brand at scale (Module 7)

Module 7 is the smallest and the most important. Read it twice. Write down what your "what if it doesn't grow?" answer is. Pre-commit publicly to one milestone post you'll publish whether the number is good or bad. That's the brand contract.

  • Watch Module 7 twice (~90 min)
  • Write your build-in-public commitment letter
  • Publish it — on a Tuesday, after the live
Finish Week 4 →
