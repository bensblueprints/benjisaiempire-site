    FAQ

    The honest answers, before you ask.

    Is this really free?

    Yes. 30 days, 30 emails, no credit card. The challenge is the product. Some emails will mention the Empire Insider tier ($10/mo) or the founder rate (Empire Builder, $99/mo locked for life for the first 50). Those are separate decisions you make after the free work is already done.

    What if I miss a day?

    No penalty. Pick up where you are. The sequence keeps going on its 24-hour cadence. If you ghost the replies for 5 days straight, the system pauses the sequence and sends a single re-engagement email asking if you're still in. Reply, the sequence resumes. Don't reply for another 48 hours, the sequence resumes anyway — momentum matters more than guilt.

    Why do I have to reply? Isn't opening enough?

    Open rates are vanity. Replies are a real engagement signal. Email opens are inflated by Apple Mail Privacy Protection and image-prefetch — I can't tell the difference between you reading the email and your inbox auto-loading the tracking pixel. A reply tells me you're real, you're paying attention, and you're making the move. Reply rates are also the strongest predictor of who finishes the challenge — people who reply in the first week finish at roughly 4x the rate of people who don't.

    What if I'm a beginner? What if I'm running an agency already?

    The challenge works for both. Beginners use the moves to build their first funnel, their first cold-call pipeline, their first AI workflow. Existing operators use the moves to retrofit their stack — replace one tool, install one prompt, ship one new asset. Either way, by Day 30 you have something new shipped.

    Will you spam me?

    One email a day for 30 days, then about one a week after that — Tuesday Cold Call Live recap, Thursday Build Day recap, the occasional founder-rate update. Every email has an unsubscribe link in the footer that works in one click and removes you for good. No resurrection sequences, no "wait, here's a coupon" win-backs unless you actively re-engage.

    What's the Hardcore badge?

    Members who finish BOTH the 30-Day AI Empire Challenge AND the 30-Day Cold Calling Challenge earn it. It's a community-profile badge that's visible across every channel — signals to other members that you've done the work twice, not just clicked through 30 emails. Most people finish neither. Some finish one. Hardcore is for the ones who finish both.
